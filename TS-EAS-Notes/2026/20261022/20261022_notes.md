# Notes 22nd of October 2026

Co-chairs Karin Bredenberg and Mary Samouelian opened the first quarterly meeting of TS-EAS year by thanking departing members and liaisons. Attendees then introduced themselves with their location and time zone, and the group took screenshots for a group photo and a map of members’ locations. Karin reviewed how the group communicates and where things are kept:



* **Mailing lists:** the SAA TS-EAS list (with tips for international members on managing delivery settings in SAA Connect), plus the EAD list hosted by Glenn at the Library of Congress, which now serves all EAS standards.
* **Google Drive:** internal working documents, rosters and meeting recordings.
* **GitHub:** public meeting notes, schemas, tag libraries and the handbook wiki.
* **Slack:** a new invite link was shared, valid for 30 days.

On membership, the call for members opened October 6. TS-EAS reported six reappointments plus two new SAA-based and two new international members, though SAA’s call lists slightly different numbers. The co-chairs plan to raise with the Appointments Committee that the TS-EAS bylaws allow reappointment, which conflicts with the new SAA language. Next year’s annual meeting will again be held online.

Most of the meeting covered the restructuring plan, which was built from members’ August feedback. The plan uses phases, because some working groups depend on others’ results. Phase 1 has four groups:



* **Data Model:** new and seen as foundational for later work such as RiC alignment and outreach.
* **Tag Libraries and Best Practices Technical:** renamed to emphasize its technical focus.
* **Best Practices Guide Editorial:** now also responsible for validating all XML examples, possibly through a technical reviewer role.
* **Workspace Governance:** new.

Co-chair roles are largely unchanged, apart from adding monitoring of GitHub issues. The plan also sets out member expectations: attend the quarterly and annual meetings, join at least one working group, comment on drafts, and contribute to outreach.

In discussion, the co-chairs said phase lengths are not yet set and the plan beyond Phase 1 may change. Everyone should join a Phase 1 group now, and members can switch when Phase 2 groups launch. Groups will be self-run and should choose a secretary so notes can be published on GitHub. The co-chairs strongly encouraged communication between groups to avoid the silos seen in the last revision, and future full-committee meetings will set aside time for this. Group sizes will be balanced.

**Next steps:** members should complete the working group form, giving a first and second choice, by the end of next week or soon after. The co-chairs will then assign groups, send out the meeting notes and begin planning a February meeting.
